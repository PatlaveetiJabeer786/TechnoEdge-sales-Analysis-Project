## 🔴 The Business Problem

<table>
<tr>
<td width="60%">

> **TechnoEdge**, a growing electronics retail company, was struggling with a **critical operational challenge** — their sales data was scattered across **36+ monthly CSV files**, making it nearly impossible to get a clear picture of business performance.

### 😓 Pain Points:
- 📂 Data locked in **36+ separate monthly files**
- ⏰ Analysts spending **hours every week** manually compiling reports
- ❌ **No single source of truth** for sales performance
- 🔍 Leadership making decisions **without real-time insights**
- 📉 Unable to identify **underperforming regions or products** quickly
